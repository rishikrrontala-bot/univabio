## Pitch, verbatim
> "Build confidence, sharpen your voice, and turn your ideas into words that truly land."

This is the tagline as quoted by the WebSearch index; I could not confirm it character-for-character. *Inspiration, paraphrased from the index:* many people have good ideas but struggle to say them confidently and clearly in class discussions, interviews, presentations and pitches. VoiceForge lets them practise by actually speaking with a live AI partner, not by typing prompts.

## The wow moment
You talk out loud with a live AI voice coach or debate opponent (an ElevenLabs conversational agent) while your webcam tracks face presence and head angle. You then get a scored session report covering both verbal and non-verbal feedback.

## Transferable to us
- **A no-code or AI-generated scaffold is not disqualifying in this network.** VoiceForge started as a MeDo export and still placed, then won again elsewhere. That matters because UnivaBio has a separate **"Best No-code AI App (Momen)"** prize and (per a third-party note) lists MeDo as a sponsor. A strong coded entry and a Momen entry compete in different lanes.
- **Stack prizes.** One submission can take a placement *and* a sponsor award. UnivaBio's Momen track is the obvious stackable prize if the concept can have a genuine Momen component. That decision needs checking against the rules.
- **Graceful degradation is a winning pattern.** Every external dependency falls back to deterministic output, so the demo never breaks for a judge. This matches our static-first, no-key rule.
- **Deterministic scoring, with LLMs only for wording**, is the right split for a health tool too: numbers come from code we can test and cite, and language models never invent the numbers.
- **Multimodal on-device signals** (webcam face landmarks alongside voice) read as technical depth to these judges.
